Tinga Tacos

" Delicious slow cooked chicken or pork with a smoky chipotle tomato onion sauce, it's served on warm tortillas with your favorite toppings. "


Ingredients

  • 2 pounds boneless, skinless chicken thighs or pork shoulder
  • 1 cup crushed fire roasted tomatoes
  • 5 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 to 3 chipotle peppers in adobo sauce, chopped
  • 1 tablespoon tomato paste
  • 1 tablespoon vegetable oil
  • 1 fresh or dried bay leaf
  • 1 teaspoon dried oregano
  • 1/2 teaspoon ground cumin
  • 1 onion, thinly sliced
  • kosher salt
  • black pepper
  • fresh sweet corn
  • 1/2 teaspoon onion powder
  • 1/2 lime, juiced
  • corn tortillas
  • sour cream


  1. Combine the chicken or pork with the cushed tomatos, garlic, chipotle chilies and abodo sauce tomato paste, vegetable oil, bay leaf, oregano, cumin, and half the onion keep the other half for after cooking. Stir and season with 2 teaspoons of kosher salt and 1 teaspoon of black pepper. Cook for 6 hours.

  2. About 15 minutes before serving, turn the heat to high and add the corn kernals, onion powder and lime juice. Cook for 10 minutes or until corn is cooked. In the meantime, warm the corn tortillas and prepare your favorite toppings.

  3. Remove bay leaf, shred the meat, modify to taste with lime juice and seasonings.
