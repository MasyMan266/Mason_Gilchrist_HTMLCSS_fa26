A paragraph is a distinct section of a piece of writing that consists of one or more sentences focused on a single main idea or topic.Key Elements of a ParagraphTopic Sentence: The first sentence that introduces the main or controlling idea.Supporting Sentences: The middle sentences that provide evidence, examples, explanations, and details.


Special Area

Concluding/Transition Sentence: The final sentence that wraps up the point or bridges into the next paragraph.Structure and FormattingLength: Can range from a single sentence (in journalism) to several sentences, typically averaging 3 to 7 sentences in academic writing.Visual Cue: Traditionally marked by an indentation on the first line or a blank line break separating it from the next block of text.
