Favorite Video Game Genres

*Not in order of preference*

  1. Massive Multiplayer Online (MMO)
  2. First-Person Shooter (FPS)
  3. JRPG (Japanese Role-Playing Game)
  4. Single-Player Offline

Massive Multiplayer Online (MMO)

MMO games are online games that suppoprt a large number of players and allows them to do quests/tasks and interact with each other in a virtual world, they often feature many different ways of progression and social interaction.

Popular MMO Games

  • World of Warcraft
  • Final Fantasy XIV
  • Path Of Exile
  • Guild Wars 2

First-Person Shooter (FPS)

FPS games are centered around gun and projectile combat that is experienced in a first person perspective as if you were in there shoes. They often are focused around repetitive competition against others in multiplayer environments, if they include multiplayer access at all. Personally, I think that most FPS games are best in multiplayer settings where skill, strategy, and teamwork are key to win the objective, when that's the goal.

Popular FPS Games

  • Call of Duty
  • Overwatch
  • Counter-Strike: Global Offensive
  • Rainbow Six Siege
  • Battlefield

JRPG (Japanese Role-Playing Game)

JRPGs are a subgenre of role-playing games that originated in Japan. They typically have turn-based combat systems, intricate storylines, and character development that will engage the player and leave them on the edge of their seat throughout the game. One thing that I have observed from JRPGs is that their fan-base is incredibly passionate and devoted to the games they bonded over personally.

Popular JRPG Games

  • Final Fantasy Series
  • Persona Series
  • Dragon Quest Series
  • Tales Series

Single-Player Offline

Single-Player Offline games are by far my favorate out of all of the genres because of the ease of play and the accessability of not needing an internet connection to enjoy them. From my experience most Offline Games are usually role-playing games that allow for deep story engagement and character development. I wouldn't say this is a genre or even a subgenre, but it is a significant category in the gaming world becasue of the lack of internet depoendability that a lot of game companies lean on for their live servers connections.
